About Us

Discover our roots, our mission, and the faces behind the Leo Club of Kiribathgoda ELPIS.

Our Story

The Leo Club of Kiribathgoda ELPIS was founded with a singular vision: to empower the youth of our community to become catalysts for positive change. Over the years, we have grown from a small group of enthusiastic volunteers into a structured, impactful organization.

We have successfully completed numerous projects spanning health, education, environment, and social welfare. Our journey is defined by the unwavering dedication of our members and the immense support of our sponsoring Lions Club.

Our Mission

To provide the youth with opportunities for self-development and leadership through impactful service projects, while fostering a spirit of fellowship and professional excellence to meet the diverse needs of society.

Our Vision

To be a premier platform for youth leadership that empowers generations to create a sustainable and impactful change in the community.
