Our Story

The Leo Club of Kiribathgoda ELPIS was founded on July 12, 2022, with a singular vision: to empower the youth of our community to become catalysts for positive change. Established with a spirit of volunteerism, our club is dedicated to making a lasting impact in Kiribathgoda and beyond.

"Elpis" means "Hope" in Greek. This name reflects our core belief: that through dedicated service and leadership, we can be a beacon of hope for those in need.

As an Omega category club under Lions Clubs International, we primarily consist of young leaders aged 18 to 30. Our journey is defined by the unwavering dedication of our members and the immense support of our sponsoring Lions Club.

We have successfully completed numerous projects spanning health, education, environment, and social welfare, striving to shape a brighter future, one act of kindness at a time.

Our Mission

To provide the youth with opportunities for self-development and leadership through impactful service projects, while fostering a spirit of fellowship and professional excellence to create a sustainable change in the community.

Our Vision

To be a premier platform for youth leadership that empowers generations to serve with integrity and create a beacon of hope for a brighter, more compassionate future.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and being a part of our Leo family.

Leo Clubs are youth organizations of Lions Clubs International. The name LEO stands for Leadership, Experience, and Opportunity. We provide young people with the opportunity to serve their communities and develop leadership skills.
Any young individual between the ages of 18 and 30 who is passionate about community service and leadership development can join us. We welcome individuals from all backgrounds who share our commitment to service.
Yes, there is a small annual membership fee which goes towards administrative costs and supporting our community service projects. This fee helps us maintain our affiliation with Leo District 306 D4 and Lions Clubs International.
We typically have one general membership meeting per month, along with various project-related meetings and service activities. Our schedule is flexible to accommodate the academic and professional lives of our members.
You gain valuable leadership experience, make lifelong friends, and have the opportunity to make a real difference in your community. Being a Leo also opens doors to international networking and personal growth opportunities.
