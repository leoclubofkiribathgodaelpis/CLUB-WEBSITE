Lions & Leos

Understanding the global movement behind our local actions.

Lions Clubs International

Lions Clubs International is the world's largest service club organization, with over 1.4 million members in more than 48,000 clubs worldwide. Founded in 1917, Lions are known for working to end preventable blindness, but also participate in a vast variety of other projects including environmental protection, disaster relief, and youth empowerment.

"We Serve" is the simple, yet profound motto of the Lions. Our sponsoring club provides us with guidance, support, and the framework to make a real difference.

The Leo Movement

The Leo Club objective is to provide the youth of the world with an opportunity for development and contribution, individually and collectively, as responsible members of the local, national and international community.

  • Leadership Leos develop skills as organizers, time makers and motivators of their peers.
  • Experience They learn how teamwork, cooperation and collaboration can bring exciting changes to their community.
  • Opportunity Leos form lifelong connections and recognize how their actions impact the world.
Leo Club of Kiribathgoda ELPIS

Empowering youth to serve communities. We are proud members of the international Leo Movement, striving to create a better world through leadership and compassion.
